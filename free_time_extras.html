The afternoon above already covers the Big Tree, the bridge viewpoint, Elephant's Walk and the sunset cruise — but Vic Falls town is small and everything below is a short taxi hop, so here's more to fill any gap or to swap in instead:

CategoryNameNotes
Offbeat & viewpoints
Date-specificLunar rainbow (moonbow)Worth flagging: the full moon falls on 26 Sep 2026, and the Rainforest opens for night tours on the three nights around it — meaning tonight, 25 Sep, is one of only three nights all year this is bookable. It needs advance booking (park entry fee applies) and runs after dark, so it would replace rather than follow the sunset cruise/dinner. A genuinely rare option if anyone wants to chase it.
Guided tourVictoria Falls Bridge catwalk tourA guided walk on the catwalk slung beneath the bridge itself — history and engineering of the 1905 structure, plus a close-up view of bungee jumpers from below. More immersive than the roadside gorge view already on today's route.
Half-dayZambezi jet boatHigh-speed boat runs through the rapids and canyon just below the Falls — a faster, wetter alternative to the calm upstream sunset cruise, for anyone who wants one adrenaline activity worked in today.
Dining + viewThe Lookout CaféPerched right on the edge of Batoka Gorge a short drive from town — open-air dining with canyon views, and the base for gorge-swing/zip-line activities if anyone wants to add one on.
Pubs & bars
BarShoestrings Backpackers & BarLaid-back backpacker bar on Parkway Drive with a regular line-up of local bands and DJs — the liveliest low-key option in town, free entry.
BarExplorers ClubAlso on Parkway Drive — a quieter, dimly-lit lounge bar built for conversation over cocktails rather than a dance floor.
Restaurant & barThe Three MonkeysPopular town-centre restaurant and bar on Adam Stander Drive — pizzas, grills and drinks, and also the usual pickup point for the Bamba Tram across the bridge if that's of interest another evening.
Dinner showThe Boma – Dinner & Drum ShowAlready flagged as an alternative to the sunset cruise — a traditional buffet with live drumming and dancing, a livelier way to close the evening if anyone still has energy after the long flights.

Gwango Heritage Resort

Dete is a small rural stop on the edge of Hwange, not a town with a nightlife scene — so free time here looks different from Vic Falls or Kasane. The good news is the resort itself is most of the entertainment:

CategoryNameNotes
On-site at Gwango
Wildlife viewingRooftop pool & waterholeGwango's rooftop pool overlooks its own elephant waterhole, with no fence between the resort and the park — guests have had elephant trunks dip into the pool from below. Worth timing a dip for late afternoon, when animals tend to come in to drink.
EveningBar, lounge & fire pitThe resort's own bar and a fire-pit area for evening gatherings — the natural spot for sundowners after the border-and-kombi day, with a good chance of watching animals at the waterhole as the light fades.
GuidedNature walk / waterhole sit-outAlready flagged on today's route — Gwango can usually arrange a short guided walk on the property or a quiet sit-out at the waterhole hide, worth booking at check-in if there's daylight left on arrival.
Nearby in Dete
Conservation centrePainted Dog Conservation CentreA rehabilitation and education centre for endangered African painted dogs, just off the A8 opposite Hwange National Park Airport — free entry (donations welcome), open 08:00–17:00 daily except the last weekend of the month. Worth it only if the kombi gets in with a couple of hours to spare before closing; otherwise it pairs naturally with tomorrow's early game drive route instead.

Livingstone is one continuous stay at Tabonina Guesthouse across both nights (27–28 Sep), so this list covers free time on either evening — most of it suits after dark, with the daylight options better saved for the midday window on 28 Sep, between the rhino walk and the Falls visit:

CategoryNameNotes
Offbeat & viewpoints
MuseumLivingstone MuseumZambia's oldest and largest museum — David Livingstone artifacts, tools, masks and jewellery, ≈3 hours for a proper visit. Open 09:00–16:30 daily; a bit of a squeeze in 28 Sep's midday gap once the rhino walk gets back, so treat it as optional rather than a given.
ShoppingMukuni Park Curio Market≈60 small stalls on Mwela Street — wood carvings, paintings, baskets. Friendly, low-pressure vendors, and bargaining is expected (start well below the asking price). Quicker than the museum, so an easier fit for 28 Sep's midday window.
CulturalMukuni VillageA 13th-century Leya settlement ≈10–15 min from most Livingstone lodges — guided visits with traditional drumming, dance and storytelling. Also a reasonable fit for 28 Sep's midday gap if there's an appetite for one more outing before the Falls.
SundownerLivingstone WaterfrontRiverside spot right on the Zambezi with sunset cocktails and live Afro-fusion bands — an easy, laid-back way to close out the evening if you're in by dusk.

Bar & live musicCafé ZambeziRestaurant by day, entertainment venue by night — live music, drumming sessions and even fire-dancing shows, alongside Caribbean/African/Zambian fusion food.
Upscale sundownerThe River ClubAn elegant, quieter riverside option for cocktails — the pick if the group wants a refined sundowner rather than a late night out after a long day of travel.
Backpacker barJollyboys / Fawlty TowersHostel-based bars with poolside happy hours and a casual, social, budget-friendly crowd — an easy low-key option if energy is limited after today's border crossings.

DAY7

Chobe River Sunrise Cruise & Free Day

Thursday, 1 October
Botswana
Overnight
Nxabii Cottages

Kasane is one continuous stay at Nxabii Cottages across all three nights (29 Sep – 1 Oct). Beyond the Chobe game drive and the two river cruises, there's genuine spare time across these three days — Kasane is small and walkable, and wildlife wanders freely through town since there's no fence between it and the park. A shortlist to fill the gaps, applicable across all three evenings:

CategoryNameNotes
Offbeat & viewpoints
WanderWildlife in townNo fence separates Kasane from Chobe NP — warthogs, banded mongoose and even elephants are regularly seen on the streets, especially early morning/late afternoon. Costs nothing, just keep a respectful distance.
LandmarkBaobab Prison TreeA large hollow baobab at the old police station, historically used as a makeshift holding cell and even a postal drop. A five-minute stop with real local history.
MarketKasane market, Hunter's MallLocal produce and crafts — dried Chobe bream, groundnuts, printed cloth. Good for curios and a feel of daily life away from the lodges.
Half-dayChobe Crocodile FarmJust outside town — a working crocodile farm with guided tours covering breeding and conservation. An easy, air-conditioned option if the day gets hot.
ViewpointKazungula BridgeThe new road-and-rail bridge over the Zambezi, a short drive from town — Africa's longest cantilever bridge, and the border area is often marketed as a four-country meeting point (Botswana, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Namibia), though the exact boundary lines there are disputed. Worth a look for the bridge itself and the river views.
Half-dayTiger fishing charterChobe River fishing trips run year-round; tiger fish are most active in the dry-season months (which includes early October). A different way to be on the water than the two scheduled cruises.
Full-day (passport needed)Impalila Island, NamibiaA short boat crossing to this river island where the Chobe and Zambezi meet — guided village and nature walks. Involves a mini border formality since it's technically Namibia, so only worth it if the free day allows the extra time.
Pubs & bars
BarKasane Savannah BarLively, central spot mixing locals and tourists — regional beers, local artwork on the walls, a good place to trade wildlife stories after a game drive.
BarSevuva Bar, Tholo CrescentQuieter and more laid-back, open 08:00–18:00 — local beers and cocktails, valued for its sunset views.
RiversideThe Old HouseOpen-air bar-restaurant right on the Chobe riverbank — breakfasts, pizzas and dinners, a relaxed sundowner setting without needing to book a cruise.
