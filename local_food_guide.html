MUST-TRY LOCAL FOOD

Africa Five Nation Tour · 24 September – 12 October 2026
Amar Raj UrsPratap RajPraveen Kumar Nallipogu

One card per day, matching Free-Time Extras' day headers. Each area's signature dishes, split into Vegetarian / Vegan / Non-Vegetarian plus Coffee & Tea, Desserts, Fruits and Indian Cuisine (for whenever the local food needs a break), with a real restaurant or spot nearby to actually try each one — researched against current listings, not guessed, but always worth confirming a menu on the night. The (≈km) figure is the approximate road distance from that day's overnight stay (for Day 0, from the Taj/Gateway area since there's no overnight that night).
