One border, no wheels. Zimbabwe → Zambia happens entirely on foot — a 20–30 min walk across the historic Victoria Falls Bridge, spray from the Falls drifting up from the gorge below. It's one of 3 land borders crossed on this trip.

The Panorama Route in South Africa is widely considered one of the most scenic drives in the world. We're covering God's Window, Pinnacle Rock, Lisbon Falls and Berlin Falls near Graskop, plus Bourke's Luck Potholes and the Three Rondavels — a ≈70 km loop into the Blyde River Canyon.
