Reading This Table

  • Nairobi (NBO) is left off this list — the Mumbai–Nairobi–Victoria Falls connection there is only about 1 hour, which isn't enough time to clear a lounge visit.
  • Johannesburg & Victoria Falls both route this itinerary through international terminals, so the paid/Priority Pass lounges listed (Aspire, Bidvest Premier, Shongololo, Mashonzha at JNB; all four at VFA) are the ones actually reachable.
  • Mumbai's Terminal 2 is where the international connection to Nairobi departs from — the Adani Lounge (Eastern Wing) is the standard Priority Pass option; the Western Wing is nicer but leans toward Business-class and alliance elites.
  • Source directories: Priority Pass, LoungePair, Plaza Premium Lounge, ThisLounge.com and airline sites — cross-check the specific lounge's current hours before relying on it during a tight connection.
  • Ratings are uneven by design, not fabricated. Where a real published score exists (Tripadvisor's own rating, an explicit reviewer score, or Skytrax's official lounge certification), that's what's shown, with its source and sample size named. Where no such score exists — most of the smaller lounges (Khami, Makuwa, Pearl at VFA; ATOL at MRU; Care by BLR) simply aren't rated anywhere public — it says "Not independently rated" rather than guessing. A few (marked "estimated" or "consensus") are a rough read of qualitative blog reviews, not an aggregated score, so treat those as directional only.
